BABELSTÅRNET — START HERE

1. Drag Babelstaarnet.app onto the Applications folder in this window.
2. Open Babelstaarnet from Applications.
3. Click “Set up Babelstårnet”.
4. In System Settings, enable Babelstårnet under Screen Recording.
5. Return to Babelstårnet. If asked, click “Enabled it? Relaunch”.
6. Click “Start hover learning”, or press fn + Z.
7. Move the pointer over a Danish word.

Everything runs on this Mac. No account is required.

If macOS says it cannot verify this preview build:
Control-click Babelstaarnet.app, choose Open, then choose Open once more.

A normally signed and notarized release opens with a regular double-click.
